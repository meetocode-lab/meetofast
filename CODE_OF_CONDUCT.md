# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Meetofast community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy open-source engineering community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Demonstrating empathy, respect, and constructive technical feedback.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping contributors succeed.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for users, security, and the open-source community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional engineering setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at `conduct@meocode.org`. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly, respecting the privacy and security of the reporter.
